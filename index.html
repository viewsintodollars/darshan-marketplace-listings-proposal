Marketplace & Quick Commerce Listings · Scope Boundaries & Terms

What the package covers, what the platforms control, and the terms.

In scope

  • Part A: Amazon and Flipkart listings
  • Part B: Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart onboarding
  • Readiness review and one master catalogue
  • Repair of existing Amazon and Flipkart listings
  • Keywords, copy, 5 images and 12 A plus modules per SKU
  • Amazon Brand Store, 7 pages
  • Platform queries, corrections and live listing QA
  • Weekly tracker and a week 16 handover pack

Out of scope

  • Platform fees, commissions, storage, returns and stock
  • Marketplace and quick commerce ads: Performance Ads proposal
  • Photography, video and packaging redesign
  • Trademark filing, barcode purchase and licences
  • Negotiating platform terms as Client's legal representative
  • SKUs, packs or barcodes changed after kick off
  • Warehousing, dispatch and payment reconciliation
  • Guaranteed approval, go live dates, rank or sales

Commercial terms

  • Part A and Part B priced per SKU; both together at the package rate
  • SKU list fixed at kick off; later SKUs at the same rate
  • Two consolidated revision rounds on copy and images
  • Client feedback within three business days, or the schedule moves
  • Seller accounts stay in Client's name and control
  • Each platform decides acceptance, terms and city rollout
  • 50% at kick off, balance at handover. Fees exclude GST
